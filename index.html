About me

I like problems where the answer has to be engineered, not just found.

I'm a senior in Computer Science at Effat University, specialising in Artificial Intelligence. My strongest work sits where AI meets real software: agents that simulate crowds, models that estimate health risk, and the dashboards and interfaces that make those results usable.

I've maintained a 4.00 GPA, was named to the President's List for 2025–2026, and presented research at Effat's Undergraduate Research Forum. Outside class I serve as Vice President of the IEEE Computer Society, lead the university's futsal club, volunteer, and care a lot about clear communication: documentation, posters and demos are part of how I ship.
